Customer Booking Flow

From landing page to submitted request — requires login

1
Landing / Services page /services

Customer browses available service types (Chemical Wash, General Cleaning, Installation, etc.) with price and estimated duration. Each card has a "Book Now" button.

If not logged in → redirected to /auth/login, then back to booking after.
2
Booking Wizard — Step 1: Service details /book

Service type is pre-selected. Customer enters number of aircon units (e.g. 2 units → system calculates 2× duration). Optional notes field (e.g. "aircon is on 3rd floor, no lift").

3
Booking Wizard — Step 2: Date & time preference

Customer picks a preferred date (calendar, no dates in the past). Then selects a preferred time slot:

🌅 Morning (8am–12pm)
☀️ Afternoon (12pm–5pm)
🌆 Evening (5pm–9pm)
These are preferences, not guaranteed slots — owner confirms the exact time when approving.
4
Booking Wizard — Step 3: Address

Customer enters their Singapore address and postal code. System geocodes it immediately (Google Geocoding API) and shows a small map pin to confirm the location is correct. Customer can correct if wrong.

Geocoding at booking time = lat/lng stored immediately, optimizer never hits a blank.
5
Review & Submit

Summary of all selections: service, units, date, preferred slot, address, estimated price. Customer confirms and submits.

→ Booking created with status PENDING
→ Email sent to customer: "We've received your booking request for [date]."
→ Admin sees new badge on dashboard
6
My Bookings page /account/bookings

Customer can track all their bookings and current status. Once approved, they see the confirmed date and time (but not which specific car/team — that's internal info).

PENDING
APPROVED
REJECTED
COMPLETED
Email touchpoints for the customer:
📬 Booking submitted → "Request received, we'll confirm soon."
✅ Approved → "Your appointment is confirmed for [date] [time]."
❌ Rejected → "Unfortunately we can't accommodate [date]. Reason: [...]."
⏰ Day-before reminder → "Reminder: your aircon service is tomorrow at [time]."
