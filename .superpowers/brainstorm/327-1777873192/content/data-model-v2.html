Three roles explained:
👤 customer — books services, views own bookings
🔧 technician — sees their car's daily job list, read-only
👑 admin — full access: approve/reject, schedule, optimize routes

Technician access (Row Level Security): A technician can only read scheduled_jobs and bookings where their car matches. They cannot write to any table. Supabase RLS policies enforce this at the database level — no way to bypass from the frontend.
